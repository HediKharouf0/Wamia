Notes:
- "0M" is the same harness with no Levee strategy; it reproduces the state-only baseline exactly.
- Latency 0 = the arb lands right behind each push (same block on mainnet); 1 = the next block (12 s).
- Guards = the v2 rules on: LeveeRateGuard before the quoter, LeveeSpendLimit after it (at most 20% of the shipped SY
  per 12 s block). The cap bound once (after manip-8, 1,000,000 SY in one block); the searcher finished the next block.
- Historical = the manipulator's exact calldata (all 11 went through); adaptive = same SY per trade, fresh slippage bounds.
- Extra-attack runs: the attacker keeps pushing in 50k SY trades after the historical attack. Only the first 4 went
  through (200k SY); Pendle rejected every later push. Those 200k SY exhausted the 5M backstop: the attacker buys YT,
  so each SY it spends sends ~20-35x as much PT into Pendle, and Levee must absorb all of it (a shield sized to the
  flow, not a wall).
